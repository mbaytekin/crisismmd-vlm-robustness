Read this before you start

  1. You are rating images only. You will not see any model prediction, any tweet text, or the ground-truth severity label. That is deliberate.
  2. Work independently. Do not discuss any image with the other reviewer, and do not compare files, until both of you have finished and exported.
  3. Every answer is yes, no, or uncertain. Use uncertain honestly; it is a real answer, not a failure. Never guess just to move on.
  4. Answer what you actually see. Do not try to be consistent with an earlier image, and do not go back to make your ratings look tidier.
  5. Approve is yes only when all three hold: the original damage judgement is still supportable, the image is still usable, and the intervention looks like the presentation it is meant to be.
  6. On unmodified photographs only two questions are asked, because there is no overlay to rate. The overlay questions are recorded as not-applicable, not as a rating.
  7. Your ratings are exported exactly as you enter them. They will not be adjusted, filtered, or replaced to fit any model result.
  8. Your image order is shuffled and differs from the other reviewer's. Progress is saved in this browser automatically; you can stop and resume.
Once you begin, the image list and the questions are fixed. If something looks wrong with the instrument itself, stop and report it rather than working around it.
